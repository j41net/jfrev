There was a pretty lengthy wait to get into this restaurant and lots of walking back and forth.

The food hit, given there were probably 7-8 of us eating we were able to try the big platter plates which worked out to be liek $30 at max per person.

The spicy jokbal was quite nice, there was also a dish that you hand to mix your hand in with rice which was quite nice.

Afterwards we were headed to the Linkin Park Concert. Given I had missed the one that was last held 13 years ago when they last visited, I was in two minds about going.

Spoke to my mate JB down in Melb who went to their show there. Despite the change in singer (RIP CHESTER) it was a really good experience.

I saw some videos from the following day performance and still managed to get some fomo from that though lol.

Despite the pricing and my initial reluctance, I am glad I went through with it.
