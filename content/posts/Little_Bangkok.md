I had never been out this way before (or at least in a very very long time).

Both Mount Druitt and Blacktown have changed A LOT. Little Bangkok resides at the top floor of Westfield Mount Druitt.

The food was quite nice and their Thai Milk Tea actually tasted real and not like some prepackaged bullshit.

I forgot my exact order. Given I was feeling sick did get some seafood Tom Yum Soup followed by some chicken dish that I could not finish so I took it home with me.

Overall despite the distance I would consider coming here again, there does seem to be a number of different options so that is quite nice.

Company is always the best though :D
