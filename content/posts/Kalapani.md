Long overdue catch up with AL. He wanted to try something Indian/Bengali food which made me realize the CBD doesn't actually have great options on this front.

Eventually I found this place and also walked by another similar venue called 'Falcha' which might be worth checking out.

We got a Thali and 16 Momos (buffalo) each with this own variation (i.e fried/steamed etc)

The food was spicier than I thought but still relatively palatable. Afterwards we hit up Ni Hao Bar for a quick drink and then let for home pretty nice and early at 9pm. Civilised boys things.

Chats with AL are always good, talked about fatherhood and other family stuff happening among us. Definitely need to link up with him soon again in order see his daughter and wife to boot.
