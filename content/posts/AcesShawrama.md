Came here for the first time to suss out the goods and catch up with PS since it had been a while. She is working over at Livo now so potentially see her a bit more often moving forward as opposed to the once a year catch ups.

The food was quiet nice, however unsure about it for its price point. The owner of the business was super friendly and is a really good business man at the end of the day.

We got a mixed plate for 2 with a side of the shawarmas. The quantity was quite nice though as there was a bit of everything. Rice, pickles, salad, 4x different skewers to boot.

Afterwards we grabbed Vietnamese Coffee Bingsu for the first time so that was quite good. I will update the name of that joint later. IT was the first time I had tried Vietnamese Coffe Bingsu so that was a treat. Surprisingly, it ended up being a little heavy (tbf this could have also been from the cheer amount of food I ate at Aces anyway)
