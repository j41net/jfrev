Came here for a feed with MH after our massage which was quite nice. I hadn't had a massage in yonks.

I had the Tom Yum noodle soup which was quite nice. However i also had like five different chicken wings prior to the massage (along with a sugar free OAK Salted Caramel drink) so unfortunately a lot of the food was wasted.

Despite this I'd do it all again anyway. The company is what was the best.
