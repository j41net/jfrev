This was a catch up with YH after 6 months. First time I travelled to her end of town in order to get some portugese food.

The sardines we got were delicious, and I had never tried anything like that. YH normally doesn't eat them but it was nice for her to play ball.

The cake/biscuit thing we got was fucking delicious however. I can definitely see myself going back for that alone.

It was actually a lot of food, and I was quite full for most of the days afterwards considering we met up for lunch.
