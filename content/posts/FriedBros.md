God damn does this place a queue. Came here during the Anzac day public holiday carried on (first time, usually they dont move it forward.)

We ended up being able to secure seats which was great. I really enjoyed the chicken and the sauce. Would be keen to try their other items if possible, the only real put off is the waiting period if I am being honest.

Came here with the glorious MH which is always a treat hehehe.
