Probably my second time eating here. Came here in the middle of Ramadan 2026 so it was really nice of the staff to offer complimetnary lentil soup and a couple of dates to break the fast for those who were not eating.

Came here with MH as this is her staple. Overall I had the steak this time and it was really nice (think last time I tried the chicken).

This place is actually quite nice and despite its price point I can see the appeal.

There was definitely some funny things happening with a family of 5 where one of the kids decided to leave because he couldn't sit next to his father, only for the oldest child to go out and fetch them back given the kid had actually left the store lmao. So cute
