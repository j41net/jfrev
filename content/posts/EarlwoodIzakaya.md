Met up with MH on this beloved public holiday since she was in the area dropping off food we went somewhere more easterly.

The initial greek place I found out was actually closed due to the public holiday.

We ended up at this place that was close by and the food was honestly quite unique and nice. We had this lamb shoulder (400g) which had a pretty spicy sauce on it but that turned out nice. The sour cream really made/offset the spice of the sauce.

We may potentially have also got free dessert but I veto'd it and wasn't sure (didn't sound free at the time).

We also went to EJs nearby for some hot chips and to use their toilet. Funnily enough a soft serve and apple pie and chips from mcdonalds to tie up the night together.
